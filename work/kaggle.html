1st Place Multiclass Image Classification

1stPlace
93.4%Accuracy

Overview

I trained three convolutional networks from scratch on low-resolution RGB images and combined them into an ensemble that finished first, with 93.4% test accuracy.

Problem

The images were small, so a single architecture was easy to overfit and hard to push past the rest of the field. The useful question was how to get diversity from models trained on the same data.

What I built

  • WideResNet and ResNet-110 models in a three-CNN ensemble
  • CutMix, Mixup, and Cutout
  • Cosine annealing
  • A 3-round pseudo-labeling loop
  • 25-pass test-time augmentation

Architecture

01 Low-resolution RGB images
02 WideResNet
03 ResNet-110
04 Ensemble + 25-pass TTA
05 Class prediction

Engineering decisions

Training from scratch kept the solution tied to this dataset instead of a large pretrained backbone. The ensemble exists because the networks fail differently. Pseudo-labeling and test-time augmentation were used only after the validation setup was fixed.

Results

1st place. 93.4% accuracy. The public score on the leaderboard screenshot is 0.934.

What I learned

On a small-image task, augmentation and a careful ensemble moved the score more than swapping in a larger model.
